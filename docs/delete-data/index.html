Delete your data in TruckoRig

Last updated: November 25, 2026

TruckoRig keeps your journal (loads, expenses, fuel, photos, scans) on your phone. The developer does not run a server that stores your journal and does not hold a copy of it. You do not create an account with the developer. You can sign in with your own Google account only to back up and sync your data to your own Google Drive.

How to delete your data

  1. In the app: open Settings → Delete all data and confirm. This erases the on-device journal, photos, scans, and the saved Telegram token.
  2. Or on the phone: Android Settings → Apps → TruckoRig → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes all data on that phone.
  3. Google Drive backup (if you turned it on): in the app, Settings → Google Drive → Disconnect. Then in Google Drive on the web open Settings → Manage apps → TruckoRig → Delete hidden app data (Google's steps). You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  4. Telegram (if you connected it): remove the bot token in Settings, and delete the chat in Telegram if you want those messages gone.
  5. Request by email: write to truckorig@gmail.com with the subject “TruckoRig data deletion”. We reply within 30 days and delete anything we hold about you, such as your support emails.

You can also delete single records (loads, expenses, photos, scans) inside the app at any time without deleting everything.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted right away: everything on the phone — journal, photos (with their GPS, date and time stamp), scans, settings, tokens.
  • Kept in your own accounts until you delete it: the hidden backup file in your Google Drive, and messages in your Telegram chat. The developer cannot access or delete files in your Google account.
  • Kept by Google, not by us: crash and diagnostic data sent by Google Play services, Maps SDK and ML Kit, under Google's own retention rules.
  • Subscription: deleting data or uninstalling does not cancel TruckoRig Premium. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Google keeps purchase records under its own policy.
  • Support emails: deleted within 30 days of your request.

Как удалить данные (по-русски)

  1. В приложении: Настройки → Удалить все данные. Стираются журнал, фото, сканы и токен Telegram на телефоне.
  2. Или очистите данные приложения в настройках Android, или удалите приложение.
  3. Google Drive: Настройки → Google Drive → Отключить, затем в Google Drive в браузере: Настройки → Управление приложениями → удалить скрытые данные.
  4. Запрос по почте: truckorig@gmail.com, ответим в течение 30 дней.

Подписка Premium при этом не отменяется — отмените её в Google Play → Платежи и подписки.
