Delete your data in TruckoRig

Last updated: November 25, 2026

TruckoRig keeps your journal (loads, expenses, fuel, photos, scans) on your phone. The developer does not run a server that stores your journal and does not hold a copy of it. The app has no accounts and no sign-in (no email login, no Google sign-in), and since version 1.6.2 it has no Google Drive sync. A backup is a file you save yourself.

How to delete your data

  1. In the app: open Settings → Delete all data and confirm. This erases the on-device journal, photos, scans, and the saved Telegram token.
  2. Or on the phone: Android Settings → Apps → TruckoRig → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes all data on that phone.
  3. Backup files you saved: delete them where you saved them (Downloads, a USB drive, your own cloud folder, a chat you sent them to).
  4. Only if you used Google Drive backup in a version before 1.6.2: that old copy is a hidden file in your own Google Drive. In Google Drive on the web open Settings → Manage apps → TruckoRig → Delete hidden app data (Google's steps), and remove the app at myaccount.google.com/connections. Version 1.6.2 and later never upload anything there.
  5. Telegram (if you connected it): remove the bot token in Settings, and delete the chat in Telegram if you want those messages gone.
  6. Request by email: write to truckorig@gmail.com with the subject “TruckoRig data deletion”. We cannot view, change, or delete the data in your app — only you can, with the steps above. We reply within 30 days and delete your support emails on request.

You can also delete single records (loads, expenses, photos, scans) inside the app at any time without deleting everything.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted right away: everything on the phone — journal, photos (with their GPS, date and time stamp), scans, settings, tokens.
  • Kept where you put it until you delete it: backup files you saved outside the app, messages in your Telegram chat, and (only from versions before 1.6.2) an old hidden copy in your Google Drive. The developer cannot access or delete them.
  • Kept by Google, not by us: crash and diagnostic data sent by Google Play services, Maps SDK and ML Kit, under Google's own retention rules.
  • Subscription: deleting data or uninstalling does not cancel TruckoRig Premium. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Google keeps purchase records under its own policy.
  • Support emails: deleted within 30 days of your request.

Как удалить данные (по-русски)

  1. В приложении: Настройки → Удалить все данные. Стираются журнал, фото, сканы и токен Telegram на телефоне.
  2. Или очистите данные приложения в настройках Android, или удалите приложение.
  3. Файлы бэкапа, которые вы сохранили, удалите там, куда их сохраняли.
  4. Только если в версии до 1.6.2 вы включали копию в Google Drive: в Google Drive в браузере откройте Настройки → Управление приложениями → TruckoRig → удалить скрытые данные. С версии 1.6.2 приложение туда ничего не отправляет.
  5. Запрос по почте: truckorig@gmail.com, ответим в течение 30 дней. Мы не можем просматривать, изменять или удалять данные в вашем приложении — это можете сделать только вы.

Подписка Premium при этом не отменяется — отмените её в Google Play → Платежи и подписки.
